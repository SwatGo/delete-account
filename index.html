Delete Your SwatGo Account

At SwatGo, we respect your privacy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below:

How to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to [ssbandaiswat@gmail.com] using the email address or phone number linked to your SwatGo account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Mention your role (Customer, Merchant, or Rider) and your registered phone number.

What data will be deleted?

Once your request is processed, we will permanently delete your personal information, including your name, phone number, email, delivery addresses, and order history from our active databases.

Retention Period:

Some data might be retained for a short period (up to 90 days) in our backup systems for legal and fraud prevention purposes, after which it will be permanently erased.
